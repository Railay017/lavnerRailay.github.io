Welcome to my website!

What is Sushi?

Sushi is Japanese cuisine that consists of mostly rice, fish, and vegetables.

How To Eat Sushi

Sushi is traditionally eaten with chopsticks. Optional sushi condiments are soy sauce and wasabi.

My Favorite Sushi Spots

My favorite sushi spots are Live Sushi and Hakashi.

How Much Sushi?

Some say otherwise, but I say that there is never too much sushi! Stuff your face as much as you want.

Why You Should Eat Sushi

Sushi is a healthy food that is low in calories and high in protein. It is a very diverse food and can be made vegetarian or with fish depending on your liking. Sushi is very tasty and there is something for everyone.
