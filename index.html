What is Sushi?

Sushi is Japanese cuisine that consists of mostly rice, fish, and vegetables.

How To Eat Sushi

Sushi is traditionally eaten with chopsticks. Optional sushi condiments are soy sauce and wasabi.

Confusing Names of Sushi

Here are some Japanese words that are used to describe sushi and their meanings:

  • Nigiri: a slice of fish on top of a small ball of rice
  • Sashimi: a slice of raw fish
  • Maki: a roll of rice and fish wrapped in seaweed
  • Temaki: a cone-shaped roll of rice and fish wrapped in seaweed
  • Uramaki: a roll of rice and fish wrapped in seaweed with the rice on the outside
  • Sake: Salmon
  • Maguro: Tuna
  • Ebi: Shrimp
  • Unagi: Freshwater eel
  • Ikura: Salmon roe
  • Tobiko: Flying fish roe
  • Hamachi: Yellowtail
  • DonBuri: A bowl of sushi

When To Eat Sushi

Sushi can be eaten at any time of the day. It is a very versatile food that is great to eat every day and every time of day.

Why You Should Eat Sushi

Sushi is a healthy food that is low in calories and high in protein. It is a very diverse food and can be made vegetarian or with fish depending on your liking. Sushi is very tasty and there is something for everyone.
